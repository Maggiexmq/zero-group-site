New preprint by Daniel Tihanyi and Giuseppe Belgioioso: [Incentive-based Fleet Rebalancing in Vehicle-Sharing Systems via Equilibrium-Constrained Optimal Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27476). Fare discounts steer users toward alternative pick-up locations; in a case study with Helsinki bike-sharing data, the scheme reduces leaving users by up to 99% relative to no incentives. [Code and data](https://github.com/tihanyidani/vehicle-sharing).
