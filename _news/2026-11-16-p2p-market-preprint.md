New preprint by Lisa Innocenti Uccini, Andrea Martin and Giuseppe Belgioioso: [Learning to Clear Peer-to-Peer Energy Markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21179). A learning-to-optimize framework that uses historical data and graph neural networks to accelerate distributed market clearing without additional communication, while preserving convergence by design. [Code](https://github.com/zero-lab-kth/learning-to-clear-P2P).
