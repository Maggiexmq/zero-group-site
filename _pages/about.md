Our research focuses on the interplay of automatic control, optimization, and game theory, with applications to complex systems, especially large-scale infrastructures such as electrical power grids and transportation networks. Our goal is to improve their efficiency, reliability, and fairness.

## Who we are

Zero Group is part of the Division of Decision and Control Systems at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm. The group is led by Assistant Professor [Giuseppe Belgioioso](https://belgioio.github.io/) and brings together PhD students, postdocs and research assistants working at the meeting point of automatic control, optimization and game theory.

We are a rapidly growing team, with everyone either formally co-advised or integrated through various collaborations.
