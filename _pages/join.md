## Unsolicited applications

If you are interested in joining the group, (1) read about [our research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) and (2) reach out by emailing Giuseppe (giubel@kth.se) with:

- your CV,
- a transcript of courses,
- a statement of research interests,
- a list of reference contacts.

He will do his best to get back to you, but the timeline may be long.

Self-funded applications through national and international scholarship programs (e.g., Marie Skłodowska-Curie, Digital Futures Postdoc Fellowships) are welcome.

## Research visits

We are open to hosting short-term research visits for motivated students and researchers whose interests align with the group.
